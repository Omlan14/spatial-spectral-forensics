# Extended figure pack — presentation evidence figures

Generated by `src/analysis_figures.py` (self-checking: it re-derives every plotted number from
`results/`, `features/features.parquet`, and `data/splits.csv` and stops on any mismatch before
drawing). Each figure exists as PNG (200 dpi, for slides) and PDF (vector, for LaTeX). Style matches
`src/report.py` (`figures/`); captions are self-contained and safe to paste under a slide image.
Supporting tables: `results/extended/`.

## 1. shortcut_anatomy — "where the separation lives"
Per-feature univariate AUROC (feature-high = fake) at C0, one panel per generator, for all 14 locked
features. Faint dashed bars show the reversed direction (feature-low = fake). Story: every
high-frequency statistic separates BigGAN near-perfectly in the reversed direction (ghost bars ≥ 0.97;
the locked bicubic resize upsampled its 128 px outputs 2×), while the two diffusion families show only
weak, feature-specific separation. The pooled "detection" signal is mostly a resizing fingerprint.

## 2. f11_reversal — "a feature's meaning can flip under JPEG"
(a) Nyquist-band ratio f11 univariate AUROC by generator across C0 → q90 → q75 → q50: BigGAN crosses
from 0.009 to 0.772 (JPEG 8×8 block edges add near-Nyquist energy to the upsampled fakes) while SD
v1.4 and ADM barely move. (b) Pooled effect sizes: f11's Cohen's d (real − fake) goes +0.80 → −0.26,
yet pooled classifier AUROC is unchanged (+0.000, E3 q50 − E1). Feature-value change and
classification change are different findings (H3's two verdicts).

## 3. effect_size_heatmap — "all features, all conditions, one view"
Cohen's d (real − fake) for the 14 features under C0, q90, q75, q50 (3,000 parents). Rows 1–7
pixel-pattern, 8–14 frequency; the red outline marks f11's sign flip. Read with finding 4: f14 (peak)
is flat at 0.00 — maximally stable, zero signal.

## 4. roc_headline — "the headline result as curves"
E5 (unseen SD v1.4 fakes in test, JPEG q75). Left: all 600 test images — combined 0.786 [0.753,
0.820]. Right: SD v1.4 fakes vs reals only — combined 0.629 [0.571, 0.690], frequency 0.568
[0.505, 0.637], pixel 0.465 [0.410, 0.527] (below the diagonal for most of its range).

## 5. score_distributions — "where the errors live"
E5 combined-arm logistic scores by class. BigGAN fakes sit far on the fake side (median +3.83); the
unseen SD v1.4 fakes (median −0.66) sit only ~0.7 above the reals (median −1.36) and ADM between —
the visible form of the generator-shift penalty and of the BigGAN carry in pooled numbers.

## 6. degradation_path — "the cost of generality"
AUROC on SD v1.4 fakes across E1 (seen, C0) → E3 q75 (seen, refit) → E4 (unseen, C0) → E5 (unseen,
q75), three arms with 95 % group-bootstrap CIs. Seen → unseen costs the combined arm −0.078
[−0.124, −0.034] and drops the pixel arm to chance; matched compression on top of shift costs −0.004.

## 7. controls_and_verdicts — "why to believe the numbers"
(a) The four control classifiers in every experiment: always-guess 0.500, history-only 0.500 in C0/C1
(1.000 in E2 by design), shuffled labels 0.503–0.521 (mean of 200), pre-receipt file facts 1.000 —
the dataset-level shortcut, exposed and excluded. (b) The five pre-registered hypothesis verdicts with
the number behind each.

## 8. arm_complementarity — "why combined wins on unseen fakes"
On the 100 unseen SD v1.4 fakes at the validation-selected threshold: pixel flags 34/100, frequency
19/100, union 41/100, both missed 59/100 — the arms flag different images, which is why adding the
7 pixel features to the frequency set raises AUROC by +0.061 [0.028, 0.094] even though pixel alone
is at chance (0.465).
